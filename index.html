About Mihir Enterprise

Driven by Trust.
Delivered with Excellence.

Mihir Enterprise is a trusted Carrying & Forwarding (CNF) partner and super stockist for Balaji Wafers, providing end-to-end warehousing, inventory management and distribution services. Since 2003, we have been committed to timely deliveries, operational efficiency and building long-term relationships with our dealers and partners across Mumbai and Odisha.

At Mihir Enterprise, our management team combines industry knowledge, operational excellence, and a customer-first approach to deliver dependable logistics, warehousing, and supply chain solutions. Guided by a commitment to quality, efficiency, and continuous improvement, we work closely with our clients to ensure timely, accurate, and cost-effective distribution services.

Mihir Enterprise is a partnership firm helmed by Mitesh Solanki, Jigna Solanki and Abhishek Solanki.
